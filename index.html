01 Our story

Perched on the hills of Antipolo, Playlist Cafe turns the whole Metro Manila skyline into your backdrop. Great coffee by day, golden sunsets by evening, and city lights until midnight.

Your favorite spot above the city.

We're the overlooking glass cafe on Sierra Madre St., Grand Heights. Bright and breezy at noon, glowing at golden hour, and cozy after dark. We pour espresso classics and house signatures, and cook hearty Filipino and comfort favorites, all set to a good playlist.

  • Coffee & signatures

    Hot and iced classics plus our Ube Series.

  • Skyline views

    Glass walls and an open-air terrace.

  • Live music

    Acoustic sets from local artists.

  • Food all day

    Breakfast, steaks, wings and pasta.
